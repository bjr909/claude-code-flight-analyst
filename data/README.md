# data/

Mock data for practice prompts.

- **`sample-routes.csv`** — 20 DEN-out city pairs with stage length and a generic "legacy_serves" flag. Numbers are illustrative only.

You don't need to bring your own data to get value out of this repo. If you ever want to test on different city pairs, just ask Claude to generate a fresh CSV — *"make me a 30-route CSV across the lower 48 with varied stage lengths"* — and it'll drop one in this folder.
